Data Assimilation Research at CIRA and NOAA/OAR/GSL

This site highlights work under the data assimilation theme, conducted primarily through the cooperative agreement between CIRA and NOAA/OAR/Global Systems Laboratory (GSL). The work includes research and development across data assimilation, machine-learning applications, verification, and diagnostic capabilities in support of NOAA’s next-generation Rapid Refresh Forecast System (RRFS).

These activities align with NOAA/OAR and GSL research priorities and strategies. More information on GSL's strategic goals is available on the GSL Strategy and Goals page. The portfolio includes research conducted through the CIRA–GSL cooperative agreement as well as standalone projects funded through other programs.
