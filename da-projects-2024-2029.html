Data Assimilation Project

Funding AgencyNOAAProject NumberNA24OARX432C0007-T3-01S005Project Budget$1,901,124Performance Period2024–2029

This project is conducted under the cooperative agreement between CIRA and NOAA/OAR/GSL. Specific tasks and sub-projects supported through this account vary by fiscal year depending on research priorities and funding availability.

The proposed data assimilation research is organized around three core objectives with strong heritage at CIRA: (1) satellite data assimilation and observing-system impact assessment; (2) coupled data assimilation; and (3) development and assessment of high-impact weather applications.
